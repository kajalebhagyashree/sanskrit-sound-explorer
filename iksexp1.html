Welcome to Sanskrit Sound Explorer

This application helps users understand and pronounce selected Sanskrit sounds by classifying them into vowels and consonants and grouping consonants according to their place of articulation.

The systematic organization of Sanskrit sounds helps in pronunciation training, language learning and speech-processing applications.

Consonants are grouped according to their articulation pattern.

Articulation Groups

Sanskrit consonants are organized according to the place where the sound is produced.

कण्ठ्य – Throat

क ख ग घ ङ

These sounds are produced using the throat region.

तालव्य – Palate

च छ ज झ ञ

These sounds are produced near the palate.

मूर्धन्य – Retroflex

ट ठ ड ढ ण

The tongue curls towards the upper part of the mouth.

दन्त्य – Teeth

त थ द ध न

These sounds are produced near the teeth.

ओष्ठ्य – Lips

प फ ब भ म

These sounds are produced using the lips.

Pronunciation Training

Select a sound and listen to its pronunciation.

Step 1: Select a Sanskrit sound.
Step 2: System identifies whether it is a vowel or consonant.
Step 3: If it is a consonant, its articulation group is identified.
Step 4: The browser speech system generates pronunciation.
Step 5: The learner listens and repeats the sound.

Example: Select क → Consonant → कण्ठ्य → Throat → Pronounce.


Applications

How systematic Sanskrit sound organization is useful in modern applications.

📚 Language Learning

The sound chart helps students learn Sanskrit pronunciation in a systematic way. Sounds can be learned according to vowels, consonants and articulation groups.

🎤 Pronunciation Training

Learners can select a sound, listen to its pronunciation and repeat it. Grouping sounds by articulation makes pronunciation practice easier.

🗣️ Speech Recognition

Organized phonetic information can help speech systems identify and process different Sanskrit sounds.

🔊 Text-to-Speech

Classification of sounds can be used to map Sanskrit characters to phonetic sounds for speech generation.

🤖 Speech Processing

A structured sound database can support pronunciation analysis, speech recognition and computational linguistic applications.

💻 Educational Software

The same classification can be implemented in digital learning platforms, pronunciation trainers and interactive Sanskrit applications.
